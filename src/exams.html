Important Guidelines

Arrival Time

Arrive at exam center 30 minutes before the scheduled time. Entry is strictly prohibited after 15 minutes of exam start time.

Required Documents

Bring valid student ID, admit card, and writing materials. No electronic devices allowed except approved calculators.

Code of Conduct

Maintain academic integrity. Any form of malpractice will result in immediate disqualification and disciplinary action.

Seating Arrangement

Seats are assigned randomly. Check the exam center layout posted online 24 hours before your exam date.
