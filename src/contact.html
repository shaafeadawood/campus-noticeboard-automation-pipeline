Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries

What are the admission deadlines?

+

Admission deadlines vary by program. Generally, applications are accepted from June to July for the fall semester. For specific dates, please contact our Admissions Office at admissions@university.edu.

How do I check my exam schedule?

+

You can view your exam schedule in the Exams section of this portal. Log in with your student credentials and navigate to "My Exam Schedule" to see your courses and exam dates.

Is there a campus tour available?

+

Yes! We offer guided campus tours on weekdays from 10 AM to 3 PM. To schedule a tour, please contact our Admissions Office at least 2 days in advance.

How can I apply for scholarships?

+

Scholarship applications are typically open from May to June. Visit the Financial Aid section of our website or contact our office for application materials and eligibility criteria.

What facilities does the campus have?

+

Our campus offers modern facilities including libraries, laboratories, sports complexes, student centers, cafeterias, and residential halls. For a complete list, please visit the Campus Facilities page.

How do I contact the Placement Cell?

+

Our Placement Cell can be reached at placement@university.edu or by phone at +1 (555) 123-4789. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 10 AM to 4 PM.
