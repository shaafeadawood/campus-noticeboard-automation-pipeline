Frequently Asked Questions

What is the admission process?

The admission is merit-based. You need to appear for the entrance examination, and based on your score and qualifications, you will be offered a seat through counselling.

Are there any scholarships available?

Yes! We offer merit-based scholarships covering up to 100% of tuition fees, need-based financial aid, and specific scholarships for sports and cultural talents.

What is the cutoff score?

The cutoff score varies each year based on the number of applicants and difficulty level of the entrance exam. Last year's cutoff was 680/700.

Can I apply for multiple programs?

Yes, you can apply for multiple programs during the application process. During counselling, you'll select your preferred program based on merit.

What about hostel facilities?

We provide excellent hostel facilities with all modern amenities. Students can apply during the admission process. Hostel fee is additional.

Is there a management quota?

Yes, 20% seats are reserved for management/minority quotas. The fee structure may differ, and merit requirements are relaxed.
